## Research plan

**Design decision:** Which information and booking steps should the prototype prioritize?

**Risk to investigate:** Homework 1 assumes customers encounter significant difficulties booking through calls or messages. Seek evidence that supports or contradicts this assumption.

**Research questions:** How do customers currently arrange appointments? What information matters when choosing a service and time? What, if anything, causes difficulty, and how do they handle it? How do they know the booking is confirmed?

**Method:** One 10–15 minute semi-structured interview about a recent, actual barber booking. One participant meets the assignment minimum; this exploratory sample cannot establish prevalence.

**Recruitment:** An adult who has recently arranged a barber appointment; ideally someone using calls or messages, without assuming they experienced a problem. Participant P1 has not yet been recruited. Record only relevant anonymous background: booking channel, device, approximate recency, and circumstances.

**Data handling:** Obtain voluntary consent. Explain the course purpose and that anonymized notes will be included in a public GitHub repository and shareable FigJam board. Allow questions to be skipped or the interview stopped. Take written notes only; no recording, names, contact details, exact appointment details, or private screenshots. Store/publish only anonymized, consented notes.

## Interview briefing

“I am researching how people arrange barber appointments for a university UX/UI project. This takes about 10–15 minutes. You may skip any question or stop at any time. I will take anonymous written notes, without recording or collecting your name or contact details. The anonymized notes will appear in my public course GitHub repository and shareable FigJam board. Are you comfortable participating on that basis?”

If consent is declined, do not collect notes; ask a different willing participant.

## Interview guide

Ask about one real booking. Do not pitch the proposed app first or suggest that the current process is difficult.

1. Tell me about the last time you arranged an appointment with a barber. Roughly how recently was it, and how did you contact them?
2. Walk me through what happened, from deciding you needed a haircut until the appointment was arranged.
3. What information did you need before choosing the service or time? How did you find it?
4. How did you find a time that worked for you? What happened if your preferred time was unavailable?
5. Was anything unclear or difficult? If so, what happened and what did you do? If everything worked well, what made it work well?
6. How did you know the appointment was confirmed? What information did you receive?
7. How did you feel at the main stages, and what caused that feeling? Is there anything else about that booking that mattered?

Neutral follow-ups: “Can you give a specific example?” “What happened next?” “What do you mean by that?”

## Evidence capture

For each answer, record a brief anonymous note linked to its question. Separate:

- **Q — Quote:** Exact words only. If paraphrased, label as a paraphrase, not a quote.
- **O — Reported event/observation:** What the participant reports happened; distinguish this from behavior directly observed by the researcher.
- **I — Interpretation:** Researcher's explanation, linked to the source note and labelled as interpretation.
