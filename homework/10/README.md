## Self-reflection
The strongest evidence is the concrete account of a past booking: it traces reply-dependent availability and waiting to a narrow design decision. The brief reported trials support basic completion and confirmation recognition, but do not establish timing, detailed behavior, full detail recall or adoption.

Next, record time and full detail recall with enough relevant participants to evaluate the original success target; test focus and validation recovery in a keyboard-enabled implementation. Component/state checks do not prove human usability gains.
